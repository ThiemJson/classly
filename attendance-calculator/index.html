How the calculator works

Attendance percentage

attendance % = attended ÷ held × 100

Attending 32 of 40 classes is 32 ÷ 40 × 100 = 80%.

How many classes can I miss?

Each class you miss still counts as held, so your percentage drops. The most you can miss while staying at your target is:

can miss = ⌊ attended × 100 ÷ target − held ⌋

With 32 of 40 and a 75% target: 32 × 100 ÷ 75 − 40 = 2.67, rounded down to 2 classes. After missing both you would be at 32 ÷ 42 = 76.2%; a third would take you to 74.4%.

How many classes do I need to attend to reach 75%?

If you are below target, every class you attend from now on raises your percentage. The number you need in a row is:

need = ⌈ (target × held − attended) ÷ (1 − target) ⌉

Here the target is written as a fraction (75% = 0.75). With 28 of 40 (70%): (0.75 × 40 − 28) ÷ 0.25 = 8 classes, which brings you to 36 of 48 = 75%.

Common questions

What attendance percentage do I need?

It depends on your school. 75% is a common minimum at many colleges, and some ask for 80% or more. Check your institution's attendance rules or ask your department, then enter that number as your target.

Do cancelled classes count?

Usually not. A class that never took place was not held, so leave it out of both numbers. Classly lets you mark a class as cancelled so it is left out automatically.

Should I work it out per subject or overall?

Most schools apply the rule to each subject separately, so a high overall percentage can still hide one subject below the limit. Calculate each subject on its own.

Why does the calculator say I can miss 0 classes?

You are at or just above your target, and missing even one more class would drop you below it. Attend the next class to build a margin.
