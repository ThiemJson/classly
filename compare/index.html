Choose by workflow

Which setup fits you?

The right choice depends on whether you want a ready-made student workflow or enjoy assembling your own system.

Best all-in-one fit

Use Classly when…

You want attendance, timetable, exams and tasks to update together without maintaining formulas or duplicating your schedule.

  • You need a clear “can I miss?” answer.
  • You want to test future attendance before deciding.
  • You prefer offline-first use without a Classly account.
Best for custom logic

Use a spreadsheet when…

You want complete control over columns, formulas and reports, and do not mind entering every change manually.

  • You already maintain a detailed workbook.
  • You need a custom institution-specific formula.
  • You are comfortable checking formula errors yourself.
Best for shared events

Use a calendar or timetable when…

Your main goal is seeing where to be and when, especially if your schedule is already shared by a college or group.

  • Events matter more than attendance totals.
  • You need shared calendar invitations.
  • You do not need absence forecasting.
Best for flexible projects

Use a general to-do app when…

Your work spans study, personal projects and other areas, and you primarily need flexible lists rather than class attendance.

  • Tasks are more important than a timetable.
  • You want project workflows beyond college.
  • You do not need subject attendance calculations.

Questions

Before you switch

Is Classly free?

Yes. Classly is free on iPhone, iPad and Android, with no ads.

Can Classly replace my attendance spreadsheet?

For day-to-day subject attendance, targets, safe absences and recovery planning, yes. Keep your spreadsheet if it also contains grades, official records or institution-specific calculations that Classly does not manage.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Classly works without a Classly account. Core timetable and attendance data stays on your device unless you choose to export, sync or back it up.

Can I start in the middle of a semester?

Yes. Enter the classes held and attended so far for each subject, then continue marking new classes in Classly.
