The attendance percentage formula

attendance percentage = attended ÷ held × 100

If 40 classes were held and you attended 32, calculate 32 ÷ 40 × 100. Your attendance is 80%.

Two more examples

If you attended 27 of 36 classes, 27 ÷ 36 × 100 gives 75%. If you attended 17 of 24, the result is 70.83%. Classly displays percentages by rounding down to two decimal places so a value just below a required threshold is not shown as if it met the threshold.

Excel and Google Sheets formula

Put classes attended in cell A2 and classes held in B2, then use =A2/B2*100. Format the result as a number, or use =A2/B2 and format the cell as a percentage.

What belongs in each number?

  • Held: every class that actually took place, including classes you missed.
  • Attended: only classes where you were present.
  • Cancelled: normally leave these out because the class did not take place.

Rules vary by institution, especially for medical leave and justified absences. Check your own policy before deciding whether a particular class belongs in either total.
