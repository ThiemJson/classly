Two ways to look at recovery

Back on target as soon as possible

attend in a row = ⌈ (target × held − attended) ÷ (1 − target) ⌉

With 20 of 30 attended (66.66%) and a 75% target: (0.75 × 30 − 20) ÷ 0.25 = 10 classes, bringing you to 30 of 40 = 75%.

On target by the end of the semester

Often you only need to be above the line when attendance is checked, usually at the end of term. Then count the classes left:

attend at least = ⌈ target × (held + left) − attended ⌉

With 20 of 30 attended and 30 classes left: 0.75 × 60 − 20 = 25 of the 30, so you can still miss 5.

When it is too late

With 20 of 40 attended and 20 classes left, even perfect attendance ends at 40 of 60 = 66.66%. In that case speak to your teacher or department early: medical certificates, condonation or make-up classes are only possible before the deadline.

To avoid ending up here, keep a running count with the bunk calculator, or let the Classly app warn you before a subject drops below target.
