Worked example with 45 periods

For a 45-period course with an 80% target, the absence allowance is floor(45 × 20%) = 9 periods. If you have already missed 3, you have 6 periods left.

When each class meeting contains 3 periods, 6 remaining periods equal 2 full meetings. If a meeting has a different length, update that field rather than treating every meeting as equal.

Use the correct attendance unit

  1. Check whether your institution records meetings, periods or clock hours.
  2. Enter the full course total in that unit.
  3. Enter the same unit for each meeting and for time already missed.
  4. Confirm your target from the current course rules.

This conversion is useful when one timetable entry contains multiple periods. Your official attendance record should decide which unit to use.
