How the exam countdown works

Choose the official exam date. The tool counts from tomorrow through that date, so an exam tomorrow is one day away. Full weeks are groups of seven calendar days; remaining days are shown separately.

Turn off the Sunday option if Sunday is a normal study day for you. Other holidays are not removed automatically, so use the result as a simple plan and mark local holidays in your timetable.

Turn the number into a study plan

  1. Confirm the date in the latest exam timetable.
  2. Divide topics across the remaining study days.
  3. Reserve the last days for review and practice.
  4. Use Classly deadline reminders so the plan stays visible.
