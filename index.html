FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Classly free?

Yes. Every feature, including Google Calendar Sync and the Home Screen widget, is free. There are no ads.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. Classly works fully offline and has no accounts. Internet is only used if you connect Google Calendar Sync, and for anonymous crash and usage diagnostics.

Does Classly use my location?

No. Classly does not request location access. Rooms and buildings are saved as text.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Yes. Create a semester backup in More → Semester Backup, save the file, and import it on the new device.

Can I import my timetable?

Yes. Paste or upload JSON or CSV, preview it, and import. You can also export your timetable as JSON, CSV or PDF.

How do I get help?

Email thiemnc.work@gmail.com or send feedback from More → Help & Support in the app.
