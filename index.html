Features

Your attendance, timetable and tasks — finally together.

Built for students who want a clear next step, not another spreadsheet.

Today, at a glance

A timeline of today's classes with rooms and notes. Mark one in a tap, or finish the day with Mark all present.

Insights per subject

See how many classes you can still miss, or how many you need to attend to get back on target.

Attendance Simulator

Try attending or missing upcoming classes before you decide. See the projected percentage and safe margin instantly.

A calendar you can fix

Correct past days, cancel a class, add an extra one, change a single day's time or mark a holiday.

Exams and assignments

Deadlines sit next to your timetable, grouped by urgency, with reminders before each one.

To-do for student life

Organize tasks into lists with reminders, repeats, subtasks and tags — with optional links back to a subject.

Today or the whole week

Switch between a focused daily timeline and a weekly timetable to spot busy days, rooms and schedule gaps.

Reminders that fit

A heads-up before class and a prompt to mark attendance when it ends. You choose how early.

Utilities that fit you

Customize the bottom bar, then use reports, imports, exports, backups, a widget and Google Calendar Sync when you need them.
