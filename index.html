Designed around your semester

Less tracking.

Your schedule changes. Your history shouldn't. AttendMate keeps each semester organized while giving you the numbers that matter right now.

Smart predictions

Know your next move before class.

The Bunk Meter translates raw attendance into a decision you can actually use: how many classes you can miss, or how many you need to attend to recover.

  • Per-subject targets and live percentages
  • What-if calculator for future classes
  • Held, attended and bunked statistics
  • Searchable, collapsible subject results
Flexible subjects

Your schedule changes. Your history stays.

Add unlimited subjects with their own acronym, color, attendance target and weekly time slots. Update a timetable without rebuilding the semester you already tracked.

  • Multiple weekly slots per subject
  • Custom acronyms, colors and targets
  • Collapsible cards for quick scanning
  • Edit schedules while preserving records
Built to save time

Import the whole timetable at once.

Paste a simple JSON timetable, preview every subject and time slot, then import everything in one go with automatic color assignment.

  • Bulk subject and schedule import
  • Preview before confirming changes
  • Copyable built-in format reference
  • Automatic unused color assignment
