Release notes

What's new in Classly

Current app metadata: version 3.7.0 (build 56) · checked 15 October 2026

A concise history of the features and fixes students will notice. Release timing can vary by store and region.

Classly 3.7

Current version metadata

  • Week now includes days off, exams and assignments alongside the timetable.
  • Optional subjects can stay out of semester totals while keeping their own attendance percentage.
  • Added Polish, Portuguese, Italian, Turkish, Russian and Indonesian — 14 languages in all.
  • Google Drive backup can restore data on a new phone.
  • Fixed notification attendance actions, widget day changes and an issue that could make the app look empty after updating.

Classly 3.6

  • Added the Week timetable grid and a complete to-do system with reminders, repeats, subtasks and tags.
  • Added customizable bottom-bar utilities and quick setups.
  • Introduced Google Drive backup and a guided checklist for new users.

Classly 3.5

  • Added Days Off planning to preview each subject's end-of-semester attendance.
  • Days off now appear in Calendar, with stronger calendar colours and improved subject filters.
  • Added a preference to turn vibration on or off.
